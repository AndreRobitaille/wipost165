## #7 About the Post

- Formal name: Robert E. Burns American Legion Post 165, Two Rivers, WI. CONFIRMED: repo docs/PURPOSE.md; Post knowledge; public Post site https://wiamericanlegionpost165.org/ and Seehafer News 12 Apr 2021 https://www.seehafernews.com/2021/04/12/two-rivers-american-legion-post-to-honor-wwi-vets-in-june/
- What the Legion is: a veterans organization built on "devotion to mutual helpfulness" and four pillars: Veterans Affairs & Rehabilitation, National Security, Americanism, Children & Youth. CONFIRMED: https://www.legion.org/about
- How Post 165's work maps to the pillars (page copy, ongoing tense):
  - Veterans Affairs & Rehabilitation: a Post Service Officer who connects veterans and families with benefits help (see #9); Buddy Checks, where members reach out to fellow veterans. CONFIRMED: PEC 29 Sep; Min 7 Jul; Min 1 Sep.
  - Americanism: the Honor Guard provides military funeral honors (shared with the local VFW) and presents the colors at community and sports events; the Post holds a public U.S. flag retirement ceremony at Paddler's Park; the Post hosts the Veterans Day Salute and takes part in Memorial Day observances. CONFIRMED: Min 7 Jul; Min 1 Sep; Min 6 Oct (draft); Events 20, 24, 31, 43.
  - Children & Youth: the Post sponsors young people to Badger Boys State and supports a local scholarship. CONFIRMED: Min 7 Jul.

It supports the Old Glory Honor Flight and turns out flag lines to welcome home deploying Guard units. CONFIRMED: Event 27; Min 1 Sep; PEC 29 Sep; Min 6 Oct (draft).

## #8 Membership

- Eligibility: at least one day of federal active duty from 7 Dec 1941 to the present (WWI is the other period), with an honorable discharge or discharge under honorable conditions. Current active duty, Title 10 Guard/Reserve, and service-academy cadets count; combat-zone service is not required; Title 32 state activation alone does not. CONFIRMED: FAQ; OG26 Eligibility appendix, p. 40ff.
- Proof: a DD214 (or orders placing you on federal active duty). Lost DD214s can be requested at https://www.archives.gov/veterans . CONFIRMED: FAQ.
- No social or honorary class: only regular annual or paid-up-for-life membership exists. CONFIRMED: FAQ.
- Annual Post dues: $45. CONFIRMED: Agenda 6 Oct; the Adjutant, Oct 2026. Each post sets its own dues. CONFIRMED: FAQ.
- Membership year: 1 Jan–31 Dec. Renewals open in the fall (OG26 says dues are payable 20 Oct in advance); unpaid on 1 Jan = delinquent, 1 Feb = suspended, 30 Jun = dropped. CONFIRMED: OG26 p. 40 ("Delinquency, resignation and transfer"); FAQ.
- Renewal: pay in person at a Post meeting, or online through the national website (https://www.legion.org/renew or https://www.mylegion.org). Members can also print a card and update their address at MyLegion.org. CONFIRMED: Commander, Oct 7 2026. Also FAQ; Agenda 6 Oct; Min 6 Oct (draft).
- How to join Post 165: come to a meeting. You fill out a paper application there and pay your dues at the meeting. CONFIRMED: Commander, Oct 7 2026. Bring your DD214 or other proof of eligibility. CONFIRMED: FAQ. Background (do not print): the Post then enters the new member through MyLegion.org, and the member gets a permanent 9-digit member ID and a card (OG26 pp. 47–48; FAQ).
- Do not present online joining as a way to join Post 165. A national website signup does not attach you to Post 165 (it places you in the Wisconsin headquarters "holding" post, which has no meetings), and the Post does not recommend it. CONFIRMED: Commander, Oct 7 2026. Holding-post fact: FAQ. If the page mentions it at all: "Joining on the national website does not make you a member of Post 165. Come to a meeting instead."
- Who handles it locally: the 1st Vice Commander leads membership and retention, and the Adjutant keeps membership records and is the members' point of contact. CONFIRMED: OG26 p. 26 (Adjutant; First Vice Commander); PEC 29 Sep (Post assigns renewals and new members to the 1st Vice and records to the Adjutant). Page should say "our membership chair" / "our Adjutant", reached through the Post email or phone.
- Paper applications: available at Post meetings. CONFIRMED: Commander, Oct 7 2026. The Post has also handed them out at its events. CONFIRMED: Min 1 Sep (SnowFest).
- Transfer in: come to a meeting or contact the Post, give your 9-digit member ID, and be ready to show a DD214. Once the Post accepts you it files the transfer with Department and National. CONFIRMED: FAQ; OG26 p. 48 ("Transfer a member").
- Transfer dues: leave prorating off the page entirely. The Post does not collect a prorated difference on transfers. CONFIRMED: Commander, Oct 7 2026. Safe page wording: "If you've already paid this year's dues to another post, you won't pay twice." (OG26 p. 40.)
- Contact: wipost165@gmail.com, (920) 860-7478, mail PO Box 11, Two Rivers, WI 54241. CONFIRMED: Readiness; https://wiamericanlegionpost165.org/contact-us
- Regular meeting: first Tuesday of each month, 6:30 p.m., Manitowoc Rifle & Pistol Club, 7227 Sandy Hill Ln, Two Rivers. CONFIRMED: Readiness; minutes 1–3; Post knowledge; public Post site.
- Skipped months: the Post does not always meet in summer (no August 2026 meeting). CONFIRMED: Min 1 Sep. Page wording: "Check the events calendar for the next meeting." That avoids promising a schedule.
- Visiting: guests are welcome at a meeting without arranging it; a public event is an easy first visit. CONFIRMED: Readiness.
- What a meeting looks like: opens with the colors, a prayer by the Chaplain, the POW/MIA empty chair, the Pledge and the Legion Preamble, then officer reports (including the Service Officer's report) and business, then closing. The Sergeant-at-Arms welcomes and introduces newcomers. CONFIRMED: minutes 1–3; PEC 29 Sep; OG26 p. 27 (service officer's report a standard part of every meeting) and Manual of Ceremonies.
- How the Post is run: a Post Executive Committee of the officers meets ahead of the monthly membership meeting to prepare business; members vote at the membership meeting. CONFIRMED: PEC 29 Sep; Post knowledge.
- Ways to take part (all optional): volunteer at the Car Show, brat fries and info booths; march with the Honor Guard; make Buddy Check calls to fellow members; join a planning group (membership, Memorial Day, fundraising/Car Show, social). Joining a group "did not commit anyone." CONFIRMED: Min 7 Jul; Min 1 Sep; Min 6 Oct (draft). Buddy Checks are a national program run at least twice a year, around the Legion birthday in March and Veterans Day. CONFIRMED: OG26 p. 26.
- No attendance or volunteer requirement exists in any record. Do not state one.
- Beyond the Post: members may attend District and Department events such as the District Fall Conference. CONFIRMED: Min 6 Oct (draft); Event 40.

## #9 Veteran help

Wording rule for this page: name the role ("our Service Officer"), never the person. Describe what our Service Officer does using the Commander's confirmed description below. Use the Officer's Guide only for background, and promise nothing beyond what the Commander confirmed.

- The role exists at Post 165, listed with the duty "veteran aid". CONFIRMED: PEC 29 Sep. The page says "our Service Officer" and gives no name. CONFIRMED: Commander, Oct 7 2026.
- What our Service Officer does (page copy): our Service Officer is a first point of contact for veterans who are stuck, or who don't know where to start with VA or other benefits, and helps them work through the system. When the County Veterans Service Office is responsible for the next step, our Service Officer often hands the veteran off to that office. This help is for any veteran, not only Post members. CONFIRMED: Commander, Oct 7 2026.
- Background, what a Post Service Officer does per the Legion (consistent with the above, do not expand the page beyond it): helps veterans and their dependents learn what rights and benefits they have, knows how to reach expert help through the Legion and through state, federal and local agencies, and gets information to full-time professionals in good time so veterans are properly represented. CONFIRMED: OG26 pp. 27–28.
- What not to promise: do not say the Post files claims, represents veterans before the VA, gives money directly, or keeps office hours. See the boundary note below.

We'll help you sort out the next step, and when a claim, appeal or benefits application is involved we'll connect you with the Manitowoc County Veterans Service Office, which handles those. Any veteran is welcome to ask, not only Post members." Built from the Commander's answers and the boundary note.

### Where the Post Service Officer's job ends and the County VSO's begins

Boundary note (for the page; plain sentences, every claim sourced):
1. Our Service Officer is a trusted local first contact who helps veterans and their families understand what benefits exist and points them to the right expert help. (OG26 pp. 27–28; Commander, Oct 7 2026.)
2. The Legion expects a Post Service Officer to pass a veteran's information to full-time professionals so the veteran is properly represented; the certified Department service officers provide the claim forms. (OG26 pp. 27–28.)
3. Every Wisconsin county must have a County Veterans Service Officer, who must be a veteran and whose legal duty is to advise county veterans about any benefits they may be entitled to and give them and their dependents all possible assistance. (Wis. Stat. § 45.80(1) and (5), https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/document/statutes/45.80)
4. The Manitowoc County Veterans Service Office helps veterans and families understand, apply for, and resolve problems with state and federal benefits. (https://manitowoccountywi.gov/departments/veteran-s-services/)
5. Wisconsin's CVSOs help people apply for VA benefits and connect them to state benefits such as the Veterans Homes and property tax credits. (WDVA, https://dva.wi.gov/resources/find-my-cvso-tvso/)
6. Under federal law, only a person recognized by VA may prepare, present or pursue a VA claim for someone else. (38 U.S.C. § 5901(a), https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5901)
7. A CVSO can be accredited when a recognized state organization recommends them and they complete VA-approved training and an exam. WDVA is a VA-recognized organization, and its Veterans Claims Section provides accreditation training and continuing education for Wisconsin's CVSOs. (38 CFR § 14.629(a)(2), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/chapter-I/part-14/subject-group-ECFRe2d861683c66a39/section-14.629 ; VA OGC accreditation listing for WDVA, https://www.va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation/accredvsodetail.asp?ID=%7BAA70F9B4-E914-449C-8F7E-0466E905DEE9%7D ; WDVA 2023–25 Biennial Report, https://doa.wi.gov/budget/SBO/2023-25%20485%20DVA%20Biennial%20Report.pdf)
8. The American Legion Department of Wisconsin also has a service office at the Milwaukee VA Regional Office. It works with your CVSO to process claims and represents veterans at hearings. (https://wilegion.org/department-service-office)
9. Bottom line: when a claim, appeal or formal benefits application is involved, our Service Officer hands off to the County VSO or another accredited representative. (Points 2, 6 and 7; Commander, Oct 7 2026.)
Not sourced, so kept out: whether the current Manitowoc County CVSO is personally VA-accredited. The county page doesn't say; check https://www.va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation/index.asp before stating it.
