# Initial public-site design

Historical summary, distilled on 2026-09-07. Superseded as a specification;
all prior design and implementation choices may be reconsidered.

The original brief served prospective members and veterans' families, community
visitors looking for activities or contact details, and existing members seeking
practical information. It tried to establish the post as a local civic institution
before asking people to join.

The first scope used Home, Events, Membership, About, and Contact, with support
through contact or events. Private resources, payment processing, and richer
program pages were deferred. This was a small-launch strategy for limited
volunteer capacity; neither those routes nor the exclusions carry forward as
requirements.

The visual intent was warm, local, dignified, and practical. The mission and Four
Pillars informed content. A low-maintenance calendar was expected to communicate
activity without a frequent news publishing obligation. Missing history,
contacts, and program evidence limited what could honestly be presented.

The lasting questions are whether people can find useful information quickly,
recognize actual local service, and participate without creating unsustainable
editorial work. The platform, layout, and publishing model have been retired.
