Before your first visit

Do I need to be a member?

You can explore a public event before deciding whether membership is right for you. Check the attendance information for the activity you have in mind.

Can I bring someone along?

Guest and children’s activities depend on the event. Check the event details or ask the Post before making plans.

How much time would I be committing?

Start with a conversation about what interests you. Ask about the time involved before taking on a responsibility.

Where does the Post meet?

The Post meets at the gun club. Ask the Post for the current meeting details before visiting.
