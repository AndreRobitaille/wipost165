Open concept on its own ↗

The idea: Two different waterways become one shared place. An illustrated local landscape gives the Post a recognizable setting without relying on a stock hero photo.

The tradeoff: The strongest local identity. Keep the veteran-service message prominent so it doesn’t feel like a tourism website.

Try the menu, event details, and “I’m a…” choices. All event dates are illustrative. No forms submit.
My starting recommendation: Made of service.

Its visual confidence feels practical and recognizably civic. Borrow the welcoming participation choices from A place for you, and consider a smaller local illustration from Where rivers meet. Keep one dominant visual idea, rather than mixing all three styles.

These illustrations are original concept artwork, not documentary photographs or an official Legion emblem. Real Post photos could support any direction after permission and quality review. The public audience and approved data boundary stay separate from the member site.
