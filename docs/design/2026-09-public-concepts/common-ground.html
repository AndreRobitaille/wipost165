Before your first visit

Do I need to be a member?

Check the event’s attendance information. For a public event, you can ask about visiting before deciding whether membership is right for you.

Can I bring someone along?

Ask whether the particular event welcomes guests or children, and what activities are planned. We want you to know what to expect before making plans.

What does getting involved mean?

Start by asking about the Post and what interests you. Talk through the time involved before taking on a responsibility.
