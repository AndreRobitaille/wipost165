# Wider patriotic and national design references

Research: September 7, 2026. The owner requested a wider search beyond veterans
and museums, with more ambitious visual references. These are discussion material;
no design direction, navigation, layout, or feature is selected by this document.
Do not narrow future work to the earlier scrapbook/club concepts.

The images below were visually inspected. Descriptions of motion and interactions
come from the cited project documentation unless explicitly stated otherwise.
These are campaign, environmental, print and web references, not all live websites.

## Paris 2024 — W Conran Design and Paris 2024

https://www.behance.net/gallery/162806373/PARIS-2024-LOOK-OF-THE-GAMES

An extensive geometric visual language across flags, venues, signage and digital
media, with references to the 1920s and the host city. The exuberant patterns make
ceremony feel contemporary. Study the complete sequence and film, not only the logo.
Image: paris-2024.webp
Original: https://mir-s3-cdn-cf.behance.net/project_modules/1400_webp/69d976162806373.678e30afa2ad4.jpg

## America250 — Chermayeff & Geismar & Haviv

https://cghnyc.com/work/project/america250

A continuous national-color ribbon extends into motion, merchandise, campaign
imagery and dimensional applications. The selected image is a designer's
visualization of a monumental application; it does not establish a real installation.
Image: america250.jpg
Original: https://wp.cghnyc.com/media/A250_Sculpture3-2820x1586.jpg

## Norwegian passports — Neue

https://neue.no/work/norwegian-passports/

National identity expressed through landscape and finely drawn surfaces. Under UV
light the landscape reveals a night scene and northern lights. The design makes a
functional security feature a memorable discovery. This is print/security design,
not a website. A possible web analogy is a meaningful visual reveal; no particular
interaction is selected for Post 165.
Image: norwegian-passport.jpg
Original: https://neue.no/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/The_Norwegian-passports-uv.jpg

## NASA Home & City — Mission and Bully!

https://www.commarts.com/project/30515/nasa-home-city
https://www.nasa.gov/stem-content/nasa-home-and-city/

The documented interactive presents a miniature three-dimensional world, with
buildings and objects as entry points to stories about space technology in daily
life. It combines spatial exploration with conventional navigation. The potential
lesson is to give visitors something to explore, with an immediate route to facts.
The live site at https://homeandcity.nasa.gov returned a blank dark viewport in our
browser attempt; use the illustrated case study for review. Live/mobile behavior
was not verified. The saved image is from the published case study, not our browser.
Image: nasa-home-city.jpg
Original: https://image2.commarts.com/images1/2/8/0/1/1/1108275_102_1160_MjkwNzcxNzEyLTE2MzgxNzU0MjY.jpg

## U.S. Soccer: Only Forward — WØRKS

https://works.studio/work/u-s-soccer-campaign

National identity expressed through athletes, supporter culture, custom lettering,
photography and stadium applications. The campaign has strong motion and typographic
components, so one still does not represent the full project. Useful for studying
belonging, energy and an identity people wear and gather around.
Image: us-soccer.jpg
Original: https://www.datocms-assets.com/136349/1767978399-works_us-soccer_only-foward_05.jpg

## Maryland Two Fifty — Mission

https://missionagency.com/work/md250-brand-identity/

The state flag provides patterns and graphic shapes, combined with stories and
photographs of residents. The case describes expanded red/yellow color and a teal
inspired by waterways. This is an additional civic-identity reference, not a
recommended palette for the Legion.
Image: maryland250.jpg
Original: https://missionagency.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/md250_8_wall.jpg

## Be Brave Like Ukraine — Banda

https://www.banda.agency/projects/bravery

A national campaign using flag colors, forceful typography, motion and public
placements. Worth comparing for visual confidence and widespread recognition.
It is heavily typographic, so it offers less help with the owner's specific concern
about text-dominated page design. No image retained from this case.

## Open questions for subsequent concept work

What could make this Post's visual identity recognizable before reading its name?
What could a visitor explore or discover? How could tradition be visible in
materials, photography, shapes or movement? These are creative prompts, not a
feature checklist. Evaluate new concepts before picking a layout.
