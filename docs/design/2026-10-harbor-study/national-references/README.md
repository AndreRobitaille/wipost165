# National imagery reference — October 7, 2026

The owner asked to study the imagery at [Legion.org](https://www.legion.org/)
and [MyLegion](https://mylegion.org/personifyebusiness/), while preserving Two
Rivers' own identity. Both homepages were opened in a browser and captured here.
These are reference screenshots, not licensed artwork for inclusion in the app.

Legion.org's current opening combines a Legionnaire in a cap and vest, veterans
greeting one another, and a stylized flag behind the composition. MyLegion opens
with Legionnaires in caps and a message about making a difference. Its other
carousel subjects are advocacy and honor/remembrance. National's community-service
reporting also shows concrete work rather than generic scenic backgrounds:
[community gardening](https://www.legion.org/information-center/news/convention/2019/august/legion-family-to-continue-community-service-projects)
and [food distribution](https://www.legion.org/information-center/news/membership/2021/march/american-legion-billboards-popping-up).

Design interpretation: caps and other service cues make the subject recognizable;
shared actions and relationships supply the meaning. The harbor communicates
place, not veteran service by itself. It should support the Legion identity and
actual published occasions rather than replace them. Adding flags alone would
supply patriotism, but would not reproduce the fellowship visible in these photos.

Current implementation keeps the previously authorized people-free illustration,
uses the explicit “Veterans serving Two Rivers” opening, and follows it with the
next live public occasion. No national photographs, unverified local service claims,
or generated Legion marks have been inserted. The question of anonymous
illustrated people in V1 was put to the owner; no answer was received during this
pass. Actual member portraits/stories remain V2 work.
