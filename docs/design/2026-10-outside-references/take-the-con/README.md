# Calibration: Take the Con — October 7, 2026

After reviewing the ten outside references, the owner said they were probably
“a little too far out there” and requested something in the middle. They again
identified their own [Take the Con](https://takethecon.org/) as a site they like.
This is the current calibration for the degree of visual invention. The earlier
ten references remain useful research, not a target level of experimentation.

Inspected the live Home and About pages at 1440px, then Home and its lower content
at 390px. See [desktop Home](home-desktop.png), [desktop About](about-desktop.png),
[phone Home](home-phone.png), [phone details](home-phone-details.png), and
[browser record](inspection.json). About navigation succeeded. No forms were
submitted. This was a visual inspection, not a complete accessibility audit.

## What the reference demonstrates

- The binder, page edges, rings, and colored section tabs create a recognizable
  physical setting. Their relationships give the content depth and hierarchy.
- The orbital navigation connects the subject to a familiar list of destinations.
  The section tabs also perform a clear navigation job.
- Labels, thin rules, stamps, captions, and the clock consistently support the
  conference's theme. Small details contribute to the whole composition.
- Readable body text occupies a continuous page. Most character comes from the
  surrounding art direction and details, without turning every paragraph into a
  competing panel.
- About uses the same environment as Home. On the phone, the large navigation and
  binder apparatus simplify while the paper edge, typographic hierarchy, and
  reading surface remain recognizable.

## Implication for Post 165

Aim for comparable visual specificity and craftsmanship with straightforward
navigation and scrolling. Material, layering, type, and meaningful details can
supply character without a game world, immersive entry sequence, or continuous
animation. These are observations about the desired balance, not newly selected
Post features.

Develop the visual language from the Post's own identity and verified context.
Keep its current messaging, Legion marks/colors, photo-free V1 scope, and practical
interaction requirements. A literal binder, space graphics, a mission clock,
fabricated archive, or a specific new palette has not been requested or approved.
The next concept should show this middle ground across Home, a practical interior,
and a phone view; the previous two V1 treatments remain rejected.
