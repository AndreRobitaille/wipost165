# Ten references outside the Legion — October 7, 2026

## Direction from the owner

The owner rejected the blue V1 card treatment and then the light-ground “Open
invitation” study: “looks the same.” The copy remains good. The next instruction
was to research roughly ten innovative, imaginative, compelling sites outside the
Legion/veterans space. Legion identity should inform our marks and colors; Legion
websites should not limit the experience.

This is research, not an approved third design. No Rails application changes were
made. The previous studies are historical explorations, not implementation targets.

## Owner calibration after review

The owner found this set “a little too far out there” and asked for a middle
ground, identifying their own [Take the Con](https://takethecon.org/) as a closer
benchmark. A [fresh desktop/phone inspection](take-the-con/README.md) records the
useful balance: distinctive material, layering, typography, and subject-specific
detail with familiar reading and navigation. This feedback supersedes treating
the experimental references below as the desired intensity or implementation plan.

## Finding

We have been solving page arrangement before developing art direction. Each pass
retained substantially the same experience: a header, a large welcome, and groups
of information below it. Changing containers and ground colors could only change
its surface. The references below broaden the choices of what a website can be.

The strongest examples make subject, composition, and behavior reinforce one
another. Poolsuite gives its features places in a coherent desktop. The Pudding
lets the reader manipulate the thing being explained. The Boat makes weather and
illustration part of reading. KIKK turns its festival theme into a moving graphic
identity. These are design observations, not claims of measured effectiveness.

## The ten

### 1. Poolsuite — a world that contains useful features

[Live site](https://poolsuite.net/) · [Music library capture](captures/poolsuite-mixtapes.png) · [Events capture](captures/poolsuite-events.png)

The retro desktop extends from the player to the dock, calendar, and mixtape
library. Opening another feature feels like entering another part of the same
place. The calendar even preserves the character when no upcoming events exist.

**Transfer:** design the relationship between navigation, event details, and
practical information as one experience. Distinct objects can carry different
jobs without becoming a repeated card grid.

**Limit:** pixel-sized controls, overlapping windows, and nostalgia are not an
automatic fit for Post visitors. Borrow the coherence, not the operating system.

### 2. KIKK Festival, 2025 edition — an identity with energy

[Archived edition](https://2025.kikk.be/) · [Capture](captures/kikk-hero.png)

The opening combines dimensional lettering, rhythmic geometry, changing color,
and a prominent date/location strip. Its theme is visually expressed before a
paragraph explains the festival. Conventional navigation remains at the edge.

**Transfer:** give Post 165 a distinctive graphic vocabulary that can appear in
navigation, dates, invitations, and transitions. A logo and palette alone do not
supply that vocabulary.

**Limit:** the volume and motion suit a creative festival. Our version needs its
own rhythm and more quiet reading space. This is the 2025 archive; its notice
points visitors to a separate 2026 site. The schedule navigation did not change
pages in this inspection, so this is not evidence of a tested schedule flow.

### 3. Oatly — personality on the practical pages

[Live site](https://www.oatly.com/) · [FAQ](https://www.oatly.com/random-answers) · [FAQ capture](captures/oatly-faq.png)

Unusual display lettering, a rough printed texture, a pointing-hand graphic, and
consistent interface details give the FAQ the same personality as the brand.
The homepage uses varied image sizes and overlapping print/browser references.

**Transfer:** First visit and Contact deserve original composition and art
direction. They should feel like designed parts of the Post, not generic utility
pages attached to an expressive homepage.

**Limit:** its irreverent tone and accordion behavior are not our brief. Our five
visit answers stay expanded, and the current Post copy remains intact.

### 4. The Pudding, “Dicing an Onion” — interaction that explains

[Essay](https://pudding.cool/2025/08/onions/) · [Before](captures/onions-diagram-before.png) · [After](captures/onions-diagram-after.png)

Onion layers form the title lettering. Further down, an “explode” control separates
a cross-section into pieces so the reader can see variation. The visual response
is the explanation, not decoration added beside it. I activated that control and
captured both states.

**Transfer:** use interaction when it makes an existing question clearer. A useful
visual relationship can replace an unnecessary paragraph or confusing transition.

**Limit:** we have a small set of straightforward visitor questions. Do not invent
a simulator, questionnaire, or extra controls just to imitate this technique.

### 5. SBS, The Boat — a continuous illustrated atmosphere

[Experience](https://www.sbs.com.au/theboat/) · [Opening](captures/boat-story.png) · [First passage](captures/boat-content.png)

Rain, layered ink drawings, brush lettering, and text inhabit the same scene.
The first passage moves into the opening illustration instead of introducing a
separate rectangular section. Chapter controls provide orientation.

**Transfer:** a page can be a composed environment. Original illustration can
supply depth and continuity without portraits or a stock photograph.

**Limit:** its dramatic story and mood belong to this work. A friendly local Post
needs a different emotional register. Long cinematic sequences should not delay
someone checking an event time. Inspection covered the opening and first passage,
not the complete narrative.

### 6. Lusion — material, motion, and depth

[Live site](https://lusion.co/) · [Opening capture](captures/lusion-contact.png)

Its moving assembly of glossy and matte forms has lighting, depth, weight, and a
controlled color system. The simple surrounding type gives the composition room.
The first impression depends on a carefully made visual asset.

**Transfer:** one excellent signature composition can do more than a dozen small
surface embellishments. Evaluate the quality of the artwork itself, as well as
layout and typography.

**Limit:** generic 3D shapes would be unrelated to our Post. This is a craft and
motion reference, not a proposal to copy its objects. The capture filename
“contact” records a click attempt; it shows the homepage, not an opened contact
view. Interior navigation was not established in this inspection.

### 7. Moooi, “Play With Peaks” — art direction around a real subject

[Product story](https://www.moooi.com/eu/story/play-with-peaks) · [Capture](captures/moooi-product-story.png)

The current story stages a sculptural sofa against water and architecture.
Material, scale, light, and a restrained overlay establish the mood. The page
shows how much a purposeful image can contribute before adding interface detail.

**Transfer:** decide what visual material will carry the idea, and invest in it.
An ordinary container cannot compensate for an absent visual subject.

**Limit:** this observed page is a calmer editorial reference, not a radical
navigation model. It depends on imagery, so its literal treatment does not fit
our photo-free V1. Older Moooi “Paper Play” experiences were described in primary
press material but were not inspected live; this capture is not of those sites.

### 8. WindowSwap — a concept understandable in one action

[Live site](https://www.window-swap.com/) · [Opened window](captures/window-swap-window.png)

One central action opens a view from somewhere else. After entry, the view occupies
nearly the whole screen, with its place and controls around the edges. The result
feels like visiting a place rather than consuming a page of promotional copy.

**Transfer:** make the site's central invitation tangible and immediately
understandable. Let the main experience carry the emotional impression.

**Limit:** do not add a video dependence or an entry gate to the Post. Event times,
contact details, and first-visit guidance must remain directly reachable. This
reference concerns focus and presence, not permission to introduce V1 photos.

### 9. Lemonade — a consistent illustration language

[Live site](https://www.lemonade.com/) · [Opening](captures/lemonade-hero.png) · [Lower page](captures/lemonade-interior.png)

Fine line drawings, a distinctive pink accent, and a quiet background make a
practical service immediately recognizable. Illustration surrounds the main
message and gives it a setting.

**Transfer:** a small, deliberate family of original illustrations can make the
entire site feel authored without requiring continuous new photography.

**Limit:** its lower page uses familiar cards and marketing sections. That is
exactly the structure we should question here. Keep this as an illustration-system
reference, not a model for our overall composition. Its illustrated people also
do not override the owner's people-free V1 direction.

### 10. Cameron's World — deliberately composed abundance

[Live collage](https://www.cameronsworld.net/) · [Capture](captures/cameron-interior.png)

The site assembles archived GeoCities material into dense, themed visual
landscapes. Scale, clustering, and overlapping images create discovery and a
strong sense of authorship. It demonstrates how far a page can depart from a
sequence of standard components.

**Transfer:** explore purposeful asymmetry, layering, and collections of related
visual material. Density can create character when the composition is intentional.

**Limit:** its chaos, tiny type, animation density, and obscure links are unsuitable
for our practical visitor paths. Do not manufacture a fake Post scrapbook or
assume we have a usable archive. This is the outer edge of the reference set.

## What should change in the next design pass

The most useful combination of lessons is **Poolsuite's coherence, KIKK's graphic
confidence, and Oatly's attention to the interior pages**. This does not prescribe
a retro desktop, festival styling, or handwritten type for the Post.

1. **Develop the visual subject first.** Choose a concrete idea connected to
   fellowship and continued service. Show its shape, material, scale, and behavior.
   A heading above an event rectangle does not yet demonstrate the idea.
2. **Explore structurally different concepts.** One could organize content as a
   place, another around an expressive moving identity, and another around a
   tangible invitation that unfolds. These should differ before color is applied.
   They are exploration families, not approved solutions or new copy.
3. **Give the identity more than colors.** Preserve the official Legion mark and
   develop a supporting Post-specific visual language: illustration, graphic forms,
   type treatment, material, and a small set of consistent motion rules. Do not
   distort the official mark or paste unrelated decoration around it.
4. **Prove an interior and a phone view with every concept.** Home, Events with an
   opened event, First visit, and Contact should visibly belong together. Include
   sparse/empty calendar content. The five answers remain expanded and useful
   links remain conventional and immediate.
5. **Make the still composition compelling.** Motion should reinforce an already
   good design. A static/reduced-motion state must preserve the invitation and
   practical paths. Do not confuse spectacle or GPU cost with originality.

For the next review, present a few genuinely different compositions with enough
artwork to judge their character. Do not rebuild all the Rails pages around
another unreviewed arrangement of the same text blocks. The V2 implementation
remains preserved; these findings do not authorize adding people to V1.

## Evidence and limits

All ten selected references were opened in a browser and their included screenshots
were inspected. This is a visual research pass, not a full accessibility,
performance, mobile, or usability audit of the external websites. Captures are
reference material only; no third-party artwork was added to application assets.
The accompanying gallery contains 14 captured views and links to all ten sources.

Successful detail observations include Poolsuite's calendar/library, Oatly's FAQ,
The Pudding's before/after diagram, The Boat's first passage, Moooi's product
story, and opening a WindowSwap view. Some browser interaction commands reported
success without changing the visible page; those were not counted as successful
navigation. In particular, KIKK schedule and Lusion contact remain unverified.

Additional candidates were screened but excluded from the ten: Species in Pieces
failed to load; The Deep Sea presented a browser verification screen; Chirpley
failed DNS resolution; Bruno Simon's current and 2019 sites stayed in loading
states; Radio Garden displayed station controls and points but its basemap did
not render. Those limits describe this research environment, not proven defects
in those products. Patatap's primary page was read but not visually inspected.

reference selector and cycling through all ten entries. See
[gallery checks](gallery-checks.json), [desktop](gallery-desktop.png), and
[phone](gallery-phone.png). The source index is [references.json](references.json).
Raw browser records and original PNG captures remain in `captures/`.

No application tests or build were rerun because no application code changed.
