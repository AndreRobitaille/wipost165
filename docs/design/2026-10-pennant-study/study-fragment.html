Your first visit

Start with hello.

Start with a public event—it’s a good way to get to know us. You can also come to a regular Post meeting.

Post165

Coming on your own?

Ask who will be there and how to find them when you arrive. Knowing one name can make the first hello easier.

Bringing someone along?

Guest and child attendance depends on the occasion. Check the event details or ask the Post before making plans. Attending an event and joining the Legion are separate questions.

Where and when does the Post meet?

You can come to a regular meeting without arranging it first. We meet on the first Tuesday of each month at 6:30 p.m. at Manitowoc Rifle & Pistol Club. For other occasions, check the location and time in the event details.

What about access or parking?

The club has a gravel parking lot and no steps. Contact the Post if you need more detail about access. Other event locations may have different arrangements.

Do I need to join first?

Ask about an occasion open to visitors. Attending a public event and becoming a member are separate steps.
